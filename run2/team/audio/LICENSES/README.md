# Audio Assets & Licensing

All sound effects, voice clips, and background music in Dragon Block Z strictly adhere to copyright and intellectual property rules:

1. **Roblox Creator Store:** Only audio uploaded under Roblox's official royalty-free Creator Store library or licensed sound packs are used.
2. **Original & CC0/CC-BY:** Custom sound designs generated using synthesis or licensed under Creative Commons 0 / Creative Commons Attribution are credited here.
3. **No Game Rips:** In accordance with `AI_RULES.md`, zero audio files ripped from commercial Dragon Ball games (FighterZ, Xenoverse, Sparking Zero, Legends) are included.
