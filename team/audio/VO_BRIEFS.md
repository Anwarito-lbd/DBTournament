# Voice & Audio Briefs (Dragon Block Z)

## 1. Announcer & Divine Tier
- **Grand Priest:**
  - Tone: Serene, composed, celestial authority. Never shouts; vocal presence reverberates across the Null Realm.
  - Key lines: "Warriors of the eight universes, let the Tournament of Power begin!", "Warrior {Name} of Universe {U} has dropped out of the ring! {Remaining} fighters remain."
- **Lord Zeno (Omni-King):**
  - Tone: High-pitched, playful, childlike innocence masking omnipotent power.
  - Key lines: "Sugoi!", "Waku waku!", "Bye-bye, Universe {U}! Pshyuuu!", "Clap clap clap!"

## 2. Universe 7 Main Cast
- **Son Goku:**
  - Tone: Energetic, enthusiastic, battle-hardened Saiyan.
  - Grunts: Sharp M1 impacts, guttural Kamehameha charge, calm Ultra Instinct focus.
- **Vegeta:**
  - Tone: Proud, fierce, aggressive royalty.
  - Grunts: Heavy percussive strikes, arrogant laughs, Final Flash roar.
- **Frieza:**
  - Tone: Elegant, haughty, sadistic emperor.
  - Grunts: High-pitched mocking chuckle ("Ho-ho-ho!"), cold Death Beam snarl.
- **Bulma (Stands):**
  - Tone: Fiery, supportive, loud megaphone cheerleader.
  - Key lines: "Go Goku!", "Vegeta, don't you dare lose!", "Finish them off, Universe 7!"
