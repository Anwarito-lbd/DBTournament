Rules: power purchases are capped at **1.35× stats** (SPEC §0.2). **Ranked normalizes all stats.** Every power item can also be earned free. Sell convenience, cosmetics and acceleration; never sell the win.
Legend: **C** = pure cosmetic · **QoL** = convenience, no combat effect · **P** = power (capped, off in Ranked).

**Pricing guidance found:**
- **Price ladder:** entry 25–75, standard 100–300, premium 500–1,000, elite 1,500–5,000 Robux.
- **Typical conversion:** about 8–15% at entry, 4–8% at standard, 1.5–4% at premium, 0.3–1.5% at elite.
- **More passes, more revenue:** games with 5+ passes are reported to earn 2.3× more.
- **Use an anchor:** an elite item makes the mid-tier items feel cheap.
- Source: rolearn.dev/insights/gamepass-pricing-ladder/ ; rolearn.dev/guidance/monetization-fundamentals/

**Battle pass:** launch around 99 Robux, then add higher tiers once there's data. A good free track is essential, and players prefer cosmetic monetization (zehn-studio26.com/glossary/battle-pass/ ; devforum.roblox.com/t/246902).

**Takeaways:**
- 2× multipliers and Time Chamber-style acceleration are proven sellers in DB games.
- Visible cosmetics (Nimbus, outfits, colours) sell at entry prices.
- Run regular sales, and track per-product analytics in Creator Hub.

## 5. Dragon Pass (season battle pass)
- **Season length:** 6–8 weeks, 50 tiers on a free track and a premium track.
- **Premium:** 399 R$. **Premium+** (25 tiers skipped plus an exclusive aura): 999 R$.
- **Earn back:** a player who finishes the premium track gets about 300 R$-equivalent Zeni back, not enough for the next pass.
- **Rewards are 100% cosmetic or QoL.** No stats.
- **Free track:** 1 character unlock, auras, titles and Zeni. It must feel rewarding.

## 6. Limited and event items
- **Weekly Zeno Erase night shop:** limited-time cosmetics.
- **Seasonal:** Halloween Janemba aura, Winter Shenron.
- **Tournament champion cosmetics:** earned only, never sold, for prestige.
- **Limited items with a stock cap:** for example 5,000 "Founder" Potara earrings at 499 R$ at launch.

## 7. Guardrails
- All characters and forms can be earned through play. Target: SSJ in the first session, any non-divine character in 2–3 weeks.
- Power purchases total ≤ 1.35× (stats, not damage multipliers), and **Ranked ignores them all**.
- Shop shows prices clearly. No pop-up during a match, at most 1 offer on the result screen per session.
- Crate odds are shown, with a pity system. No paid gacha for power items.
- Run 20–30% sales to test prices, and watch per-product analytics.
