## 3. Team Character Budget: The Anime Balancing Solution

In the 40-Player Tournament of Power, players pick their fighters within a **Team Power Budget** ($3 	imes 	ext{teamSize} + 1 = 16	ext{ points}$ for a 5-player team):

| Tier | Point Cost | Team Limit | Attributes & Mechanics |
|---|---|---|---|
| **EX / Grand Priest** | 9 pts | Max 1 (Event mode) | Reality-bending gravity pulls, Allied rewinds. |
| **Angel** | 7 pts | Max 1 per team | Autonomous Ultra Instinct, Staff Warp, Temporal Rewind. |
| **God of Destruction** | 6 pts | Max 1–2 per team | Destroyer Aura (-20% ki blast dmg), Hakai Execution. |
| **Tier S / S+** | 4 pts | Max 2 per team | High base PL (1,100–1,200), multi-stage forms. |
| **Tier A** | 3 pts | Unlimited | Balanced all-rounders (PL 900–1,050). |
| **Tier B** | 2 pts | Unlimited | Fast ki regen, agile utility kits (PL 750–850). |
| **Tier C** | 1 pt | Unlimited | Support buffs, Solar Flare blinds, rapid recovery. |

> [!NOTE]
> **Why this system works:** If a player chooses to play as an Angel (7 pts) or Destroyer (6 pts), their team's remaining budget is constrained, forcing teammates to pick tactical C- and B-tier support fighters (like Krillin for Solar Flare or Roshi for Mafuba). This **directly reflects the anime**, where Universe 7 relied on coordinated teamwork between aces and support fighters!

## 4. Distinct Playstyles, Combos & Movesets

### 4.1 The Angels (Whis, Vados, Marcarita)
- **Combat Style:** Fluid, effortless staff martial arts. Never gets dirty; attacks flow with circular sidesteps.
- **Passive — Autonomous Ultra Instinct:**
  - $35\%$ innate chance to auto-vanish any incoming light attack (costs 8 Ki per dodge with 1.5s internal cooldown).
- **Signature Kit:**
  - `StaffWarp` (Cooldown 8s, 15 Ki): Teleports behind the attacker in a blur and delivers a concussive tap to the crown of the head ($0.2	ext{ s}$ stun).
  - `TemporalRewind` (Cooldown 35s, 40 Ki): Rewinds time for the user by $3	ext{ seconds}$, restoring health lost in that window.
  - `AngelicBarrier` (Cooldown 20s, 35 Ki): Creates an impenetrable $360^\circ$ crystalline dome that deflects all enemy beams and projectiles for $3	ext{ seconds}$.

---

### 4.2 The Gods of Destruction (Beerus, Champa, Belmod)
- **Combat Style:** Heavy, arrogant, devastating strikes with sweeping claw slashes and explosive destruction energy.
- **Passive — Destroyer Aura:**
  - Takes $-20\%$ damage from all incoming ki blasts and beams; gains hyper-armor against light hitstun.
- **Signature Kit:**
  - `DestructionSphere` (Cooldown 10s, 35 Ki): Launches a tracking purple sphere of destruction that detonates on contact, cracking the arena.
  - `GodOfDestructionWrath` (Cooldown 16s, 25 Ki): Emits a concussive burst of divine destruction pressure knocking back all nearby melee attackers by $40	ext{ studs}$.
  - `Hakai` (Ultimate, Cooldown 45s, 60 Ki):
    - *In PvP:* Deals 20 damage compressed, but if the target is **below 15% HP**, Hakai immediately executes and erases them into purple particle embers.
    - *Against Raid Bosses:* Deals massive 70 true destruction damage.

---

### 4.3 Broly (DBS) — Legendary Super Saiyan
- **Combat Style:** Unstoppable brute force, ground pounds, and screen-shaking grappling slams.
- **Passive — Wrathful Rage:**
  - As Broly takes damage, his attack power increases by up to $+15\%$ (within the $1.35	imes$ cap), and his M1 hits gain $+2$ frames of heavy hitstop.
- **Signature Kit:**
  - `GiganticRoar`: Unfurls a seismic mouth beam causing intense camera shake and radial knockback.
  - `PlanetCrusher`: Leaps into the stratosphere and hurls a massive emerald meteor into the center of the arena.
  - `EraserCannon`: Concentrated green ki sphere with intense shield-piercing guard damage.

---

### 4.4 Future Trunks & Goku Black
- **Future Trunks:**
  - Fast-paced dual-handed sword combos with aerial launcher enders.
  - Specials: `ShiningSwordAttack`, `BurningAttack`, and the `SwordOfHope` light-blade ultimate.
- **Goku Black:**
  - Sadistic, mocking demeanor with Ki blade piercing attacks.
  - Specials: `GodSlicer` (instant ki-blade dash strike), `BlackKamehameha`, and `DivineLasso` (exploding energy needles).

## 5. Competitive Ranked Normalization

- **In Casual / ToP Battle Royale:** Lore power scaling, form ladders, and budget trade-offs create spectacle and anime immersion.
- **In 1v1 & 3v3 Ranked Mode:**
  - Every fighter's base stats are normalized to an **exact 1:1 ratio** ($PL = 3,500$).
  - Divine picks and energy drinks are restricted.
  - Pure skill, combo execution, parry timing ($0.2	ext{ s}$ window), and Vanish management determine the champion!
